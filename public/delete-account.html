Delete your Gymblix account

Applies to the Gymblix app by Gymblix

You can delete your Gymblix account at any time, either from inside the app or by emailing us. This page explains both options and what happens to your data.

Option 1: Delete from the app

  1. Open the Gymblix app and sign in.
  2. Go to More → My Profile → Privacy & Security.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm. You will be signed out straight away.

Option 2: Ask us by email

If you can no longer open the app, email gymblix.app@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my Gymblix account". Include your gym's name. We will confirm and process the request within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Gym owner (Admin): deleting your account deletes the whole gym: the gym profile, all staff accounts, members, memberships and packages, payments and invoices, attendance, expenses, salaries, supplements and stock, leads, and uploaded photos and receipts.
  • Manager or Trainer: only your own staff account and login are deleted. The gym's records stay with the gym, but your name is removed from them.

When it is deleted

Your account is deactivated immediately and nobody can sign in to it. All data listed above is then permanently erased after a 30-day grace period. If you deleted your account by mistake, email us within those 30 days and we can restore it.

What we may keep

Google Play keeps its own records of subscription purchases, under Google's privacy policy. We do not keep any other copy of your data after the 30 days.

Gym members don't have their own Gymblix login. If you are a gym member and want your data removed, ask your gym. Or email us and we will pass your request to the gym.

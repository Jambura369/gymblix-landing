Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

Gymblix ("we", "us", "our") provides a gym management platform used by gym owners, staff (Admins, Managers, Trainers), and their members through our mobile app and web dashboard (together, the "Service"). This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Who this applies to

This policy covers two kinds of users:

  • Gym staff (Admins, Managers, Trainers) who create an account, run the app, and manage their gym's operations and subscription.
  • Gym members whose data (name, contact info, membership, attendance, payment records) is entered into the Service by their gym's staff in order to operate the gym.

If you are a gym member and have a question about your data, please contact your gym directly — they control what is entered into the Service on your behalf. Gym staff can reach us using the contact details below for account-level requests.

2. Information we collect

CategoryExamples
Account & profileName, email, phone number, role, profile photo, gym name/logo/address

We do not collect precise location data, and we do not access your device's contacts, call log, or SMS messages.

3. How we use this information

  • To provide the core Service — managing members, attendance, payments, staff, and reports.
  • To operate the gym-owner subscription (plan limits, billing history, renewal reminders).
  • To send push notifications relevant to your account (e.g. renewal alerts, verification requests).
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and diagnose crashes or bugs.
  • To respond to support requests.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use member data for advertising.

4. Payments

Gym membership fees paid by members to their gym are collected directly by the gym (cash or the gym's own UPI/QR code) — the app is only used to record that a payment was made; we do not process or store card/bank details for these payments.

A gym's subscription to Gymblix itself (to unlock app features) is billed through Google Play Billing on Android. Google processes that payment; we receive only the subscription status and a purchase reference from Google, never your card details.

6. Data retention

We retain account and operational data for as long as the gym's account is active, so the Service continues to work correctly. When an account is deleted, it is deactivated immediately and its data is permanently erased after a 30-day grace period (see how to delete your account).

7. Your choices

  • You can update your profile information from within the app at any time.
  • You can delete your account and associated data from the app or by contacting us — see how to delete your account.
  • You can disable push notifications from your device settings.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures (encrypted connections, access controls, and row-level database security) to protect data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information appropriately.

9. Children's privacy

The Service is intended for gym businesses and their adult staff/members. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children under 13.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective date" above.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to gymblix.app@gmail.com.

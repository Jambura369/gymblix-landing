Questions gym owners ask

Is my gym's data safe?

Yes. Your data is stored securely in the cloud, not just on your phone, so a lost or broken phone loses nothing. Only you and the staff you add can see your gym's data, and you can download all your members and payments as a file any time.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. Gymblix is an Android app today. Your staff need Android phones to use it.

What happens after the 30-day free trial?

You choose a plan to keep using Gymblix. No card is needed for the trial, so you are never charged without choosing a plan yourself.

My trainers are not good with phones. Can they use it?

If they can use WhatsApp, they can use Gymblix. We help you and your staff get started over WhatsApp or a call.

I already have my members in a register or Excel. Do I start from zero?

No. If your list is in Excel, import it in one go. If it is in a register, add members as they renew, and the app fills up within a month.

Does it need internet?

Yes, it needs a normal mobile data or Wi-Fi connection, the same as WhatsApp.

How do I pay, and can I cancel?

Plans are paid monthly or yearly through Google Play. You can cancel any time from the Play Store. See our refund policy.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

This policy explains how Gymblix subscriptions are billed, how to cancel, and when you can get a refund. Gymblix is a software service, so there are no physical goods to return.

1. Free trial

Every new gym gets a 30-day free trial. You don't need a card for the trial and you are not charged when it ends. You only pay if you choose a paid plan.

2. Paid plans and billing

  • Paid plans (Starter, Growth, Elite) are monthly or yearly subscriptions.
  • In the Android app, payments are processed by Google Play using the payment method on your Google account.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period unless you cancel.
  • When you upgrade or downgrade, Google Play adjusts the charge for the rest of your billing period.

3. How to cancel

  1. Open the Google Play Store app on your phone.
  2. Tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  3. Select Gymblix and tap Cancel subscription.

You can cancel at any time. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for, and you will not be charged again. After that, the plan's features are locked until you subscribe again. Your gym's data is kept, so you can pick up where you left off.

Uninstalling the app or deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Please cancel it in Google Play as shown above.

4. Refunds

Within 48 hours of a charge

You can request a refund directly from Google. Open Google Play order history, find the Gymblix charge, and choose Request a refund. Google decides these requests under the Google Play refund policy.

After 48 hours

Email us at gymblix.app@gmail.com. We give a full refund for:

  • Duplicate charges for the same billing period.
  • Being charged after you had already cancelled.
  • A technical problem on our side that stopped you from using paid features, which we could not fix within 7 days of your report.

Apart from these cases, we don't give refunds or partial refunds for the unused part of a billing period. When you cancel, you keep access until the period ends.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. For Google Play purchases, Google processes the refund. It usually arrives within 3–5 business days, depending on your bank or card.

5. Gym membership fees

Gymblix helps gyms record the fees their members pay. Gymblix does not collect or hold those fees. If you are a gym member, please contact your gym about membership refunds.

6. Contact us

For any billing question, email gymblix.app@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp. Please include your gym's name and the email or phone number on your account.
